How to Download YouTube Videos & MP3 Audio

  1. Step 1: Copy the video or audio web address (URL) from YouTube or supported platforms.
  2. Step 2: Paste the copied video link into the URL input box above.
  3. Step 3: Click "Fetch Options" to display supported HD formats and bitrates.
  4. Step 4: Select either Video MP4 (1080p/720p) or Audio MP3 (320kbps).
  5. Step 5: Click "Download File" to convert and save high-speed media to your mobile or PC.
